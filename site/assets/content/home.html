Agentic capabilities

Every action happens inside OneDash.

Not a dashboard that tells your team what to go do somewhere else. The decision and the action live in the same place — and an agent can take the action for you.

01 Agents that make contact
  • AI voice agents for patientsDial the whole cohort in parallel, work the protocol your team would work, and warm-transfer to a person the moment a member is ready to act. Calls stay inside FCC-allowable windows automatically.
  • AI voice agents for providersNavigate the office IVR, get to the right desk, and confirm whether a member was actually seen — the follow-up call nobody has time to make.
  • AI voice agents for pharmaciesRequest the 90-day refill, check a fill status, find out what is blocking the pickup. Any use case where the answer is a phone call.
02 Every other channel, same screen
  • Bidirectional textingReal conversations, not blast campaigns. Replies land in a threaded inbox, and your team can pick up a thread the agent started.
  • Fax, including bulkStill how most provider offices want to be reached. Send one from the gap itself, or send the whole list at once.
  • EHR messages over Direct TrustStraight into the provider's chart — routed by taxonomy, or to the clinician who last wrote the prescription.
03 Your rules, and the human override
  • Automations and rulesBuild the pathway once — which members, which channel, in what order, how long between attempts, which days to skip — and OneDash runs it for the rest of the year.
  • Human in the loop, alwaysA caseworker can open any member, edit anything on the fly, take an action by hand, or pull someone off a pathway entirely. The agents work for your team, not around it.
  • An audit trail on everythingEvery call, text, fax, and EHR message is a line item on the gap it was meant to close — who sent it, when, and what came back.

Why it matters — by who's asking

The stakes change depending on where you sit.

A half-star slide can wipe out your Quality Bonus Payment — and you'd learn it a year too late.

Cut points move every year — a measure comfortably above threshold in January can slip below it by the time CMS scores you, with nothing left to work. The plans that hold their rating watch performance against the cut point during the year, while members can still be moved.

How OneDash answers it

Measure-level performance against live cut points — not a year-end surprise.

OneDash works alongside your certified CMS/HEDIS measure vendor — a real-time operational layer that sharpens the numbers you already report, not a replacement for them.

  • Real-time cut-point trackingDaily feeds re-score every clinical Stars and HEDIS measure against current thresholds — so you see half-star risk while you can still close it. Real-time, not just faster reporting.
  • Refined, real-time denominatorsCleans up who's actually in each measure and who's already met it — so your team stops chasing gaps that are already closed.
  • Members who can still flipTargeted lists of the exact members who move a measure above threshold — ranked by impact on your rating.

Three triple-weighted adherence measures decide your Part D score. Missing one isn't a rounding error.

Diabetes, hypertension, and cholesterol adherence carry triple weight — any one can pull your summary rating down. And CMR isn't optional: you have to hit the rate and prove it. By hand, in Q4, that burns out pharmacists and still falls short.

How OneDash answers it

Hit the heavy-weighted measures — and prove CMR compliance — without the manual scramble.

  • PDC tracked per member, per measureMAD, MAH, and MAC adherence monitored continuously, with automated refill and outreach sequences before a member falls below 80%.
  • CMR completion, trackedMembers' CMR appointments booked automatically with your pharmacists — completion tracked and documented inside OneDash.
  • Agent-worked adherence outreachBefore a member lapses, an agent texts them, calls them, and calls the pharmacy for the refill — so your pharmacists work the exceptions, not the whole panel.

Your MLR and your Star Rating set your AEP position. Both erode when gaps close late.

Late gap closure hits your books twice — avoidable medical spend that pushes MLR the wrong way, and forfeited Quality Bonus revenue when the rating slips. Walk into AEP soft on both and you're defending share, not taking it.

How OneDash answers it

Every closed gap tied to a dollar — quality revenue protected and medical spend avoided.

It's not just quality gaps. OneDash closes spend gaps too — high utilizers, drug-utilization mismatches, avoidable admissions — dollars most plans never have the data or people to chase.

The agentic roadmap

This isn't a one-time automation project. A campaign starts user-worked, moves to agent-assisted once the protocol is proven, and ends up agent-worked with your team on the exceptions.
